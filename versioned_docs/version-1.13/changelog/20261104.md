We added our response policy for critical vulnerabilities to the documentation.
The policy describes our approach to service continuity, the order in which functions may be suspended, and how we communicate with customers.
As part of operating SaaSus Platform, we take the following ongoing measures:

- Track the software and versions we use
- Automate tests and the application of fixes
- Prepare mechanisms to suspend functions individually
- Establish emergency decision-making structures and procedures, and conduct regular drills

For details, see the [Response Policy for Critical Vulnerabilities](https://docs.saasus.io/docs/part-8/vulnerability-response-policy).
