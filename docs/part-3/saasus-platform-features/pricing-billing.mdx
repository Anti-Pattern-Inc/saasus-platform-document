## Pricing structure design

Create pricing plans that combine fixed monthly charges with usage-based charges such as API call counts.  
Build plans using meter units to record usage, pricing units to define charges, and feature menus to group pricing units. Apply the resulting plan and tax rate to a tenant.

### Reset pricing plans

Resetting deletes the following information in the selected environment:

- Pricing plans, feature menus, pricing units, meter units, and tax rates
- Recorded usage
- Scheduled plan changes and plan histories for all tenants

If Stripe integration is configured, resetting also removes the integration mappings and secret key from SaaSus Platform and disconnects Stripe.  
Products, customers, and subscriptions in Stripe are not deleted.

Scheduled changes cannot be modified or canceled from five minutes before their effective time.  
With Stripe integration, a scheduled change that removes metered subscription items (`delete_usage`) cannot be canceled.

### Prorations when changing plans

With Stripe integration, pricing plan changes support prorations.  
Changes made through the SaaS Operations Console invoice the prorated difference immediately.

Use `proration_behavior` in [UpdateTenantPlan](/docs/reference/auth-api#tag/tenant/operation/UpdateTenantPlan) to specify whether to prorate and when to invoice the difference.

- `always_invoice`: Issue an invoice for the prorated difference immediately.
- `create_prorations`: Include the prorated difference in the next invoice.
- `none`: Do not prorate the plan change.

#### Example with fixed monthly prices

Suppose a tenant switches from a JPY 25,000 monthly plan to a JPY 28,000 monthly plan halfway through the billing period. Excluding tax and usage-based charges, the difference is:

- Unused time on the old plan: −JPY 12,500
- Remaining time on the new plan: +JPY 14,000
- Difference: JPY 1,500

For the reverse change from JPY 28,000 to JPY 25,000 per month, the −JPY 1,500 difference is retained as a Stripe credit and applied to future invoices.  
Stripe calculates the actual amounts to the second based on the change time and billing period.
